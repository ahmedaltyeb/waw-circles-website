## Reusable public structure

1. **Executive summary:** who needed what, WAW's verified contribution and evidenced result. If no result data is available, describe only confirmed delivery.
2. **Client and sector:** approved identity or honest anonymized description. No invented client persona presented as fact.
3. **Business context:** operating environment and existing process supported by interviews or documents.
4. **Challenge:** verified pain points; distinguish client reports from measured observations.
5. **Objectives and constraints:** success definition, scope boundaries, timeline/budget constraints only when approved for disclosure.
6. **Proposed solution:** explain the agreed approach and rationale. Separate proposals from delivered work.
7. **Features and workflows delivered:** verified user journeys, responsibilities and screenshots; omit speculative features.
8. **Technology stack:** publish only confirmed platforms/integrations with permission; omit sensitive architecture.
9. **WAW responsibilities:** design, development, integration, testing or support only as evidenced; credit collaborators as approved.
10. **QA and testing approach:** verified coverage, test methods, limitations and acceptance process; no unsupported quality guarantees.
11. **Results:** quantitative claims require baseline, final measure, unit, period, sample/source, attribution limits and permission. Qualitative outcomes require an approved source. Omit this section if unsupported; do not replace it with hypothetical results.
12. **Lessons learned:** approved reflection without disclosing confidential client information.
13. **Related services/projects:** validated published IDs only.
14. **CTA:** “Discuss a similar project / ناقش مشروعاً مشابهاً” → localized Contact.

Required public core: sections 1–7, 9 and 14. Sections 8, 10–13 are conditional on evidence. If the core cannot be substantiated, keep the whole case study unpublished. Editorial TODOs never render publicly.

## Publication checklist

- [ ] Each factual claim traces to supplied or verified evidence.
- [ ] Client name, logo, images, outcome claims and quotes have explicit permission.
- [ ] Confidential data, account details and personal information are removed from screenshots.
- [ ] Proposal, delivered functionality and future roadmap are clearly distinguished.
- [ ] Both languages carry equivalent claims, status and CTA; proper names are approved.
- [ ] Approved SEO, canonical/hreflang, captions and alt text exist.
- [ ] Related links resolve; cards correctly identify classification.
