## 2. What We Do

We help businesses move from idea to launch and beyond: think and design the right experience, build and connect the systems, then support and improve after launch.

### Think & Design
- **Product strategy and technology consultancy:** business needs, product roadmaps, first-release planning and technology selection.
- **Customer journey and experience design:** journey mapping, onboarding, purchase and support flows; finding friction and making each touchpoint more useful.

### Build & Connect
- **Websites and e-commerce:** company websites, online stores, customer portals and web platforms.
- **Mobile app design and development:** mobile and web apps, from prototype to store release.
- **Applications and business systems:** operations tools, dashboards and tested prototypes.
- **Connected systems and automation:** integrations and workflows that reduce repetitive steps, with AI-assisted tools where they add value.
- **Software testing and quality assurance (QA).**

### Support & Continuity
- **Technical support and continuous improvement:** software troubleshooting, maintenance, updates and user guidance, within an agreed scope.

## 3. How We Work

| Step | Stage | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Listen & discover (نسمع ونفهم) | Understand goals and users |
| 2 | Shape & agree (نخطط ونتفق) | Agree scope, costs and milestones |
| 3 | Build & test (نطوّر ونختبر) | Design, develop and review |
| 4 | Launch & improve (نطلق ونواصل التحسين) | Handover and agreed support |

### Where clients start
- **START:** a new idea. A clear plan, a convincing prototype or a first release.
- **GROW:** ready for more. A stronger digital presence, connected tools and better journeys.
- **IMPROVE:** making an existing website, app or workflow better with the right changes.
